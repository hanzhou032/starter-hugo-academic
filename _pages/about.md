I am a final-year PhD student in _Computation, Cognition, and Language_ (NLP) at [Language Technology Lab](https://ltl.mmll.cam.ac.uk/), [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/). I am supervised by [Prof. Anna Korhonen](https://sites.google.com/site/annakorhonen/) and [Dr. Ivan Vulić](https://sites.google.com/site/ivanvulic/). My research interests are all about reward-driven intelligence. Previously, I interned as an AI Scientist Intern at [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/) and as a Student Researcher at [Google DeepMind](https://www.deepmind.com/), [Google Cloud AI Research](https://research.google/), and [Google Research](https://research.google/).

**I am looking for full-time Research Scientist roles.** Feel free to contact me for any research collaboration and potential job opportunities.

**Interests:** LLMs · Reinforcement Learning · Reasoning · Visual Intelligence
